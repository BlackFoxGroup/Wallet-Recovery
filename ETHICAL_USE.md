# Ethical use and responsibility

Wallet Recovery is meant to help rightful owners regain access to their own digital assets after forgetting recovery phrase words. Use it only for a wallet you own or are explicitly authorized to recover.

Never use the software to access someone else's wallet or to obtain, disclose, or misuse another person's recovery phrase, seed, or private key. Protect all recovery information and keep it off public issues, chats, and support requests.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, obtaining permission where needed, and safeguarding their own data. Black Fox Group promotes ethical use and respects privacy and ownership. This statement describes the intended use; it does not replace the user's responsibility to act lawfully and ethically.
